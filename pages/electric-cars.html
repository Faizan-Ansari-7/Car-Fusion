Balanced View

Advantages vs Challenges

Advantages (Pros)

  • Lower Running Costs: Electricity is roughly 1/10th the cost of petrol per km.
  • Minimal Maintenance: No oil changes, no spark plugs, no transmission fluids. Fewer moving parts mean fewer breakdowns.
  • Government Subsidies: Tax benefits (Section 80EEB) and FAME II subsidies reduce upfront costs in India.
  • Performance: Superior acceleration and handling due to a low center of gravity (battery placement).

Challenges (Cons)

  • Range Anxiety: Although improving, the fear of running out of charge on long highway trips still exists for some users.
  • Charging Infrastructure: While cities like Surat and Mumbai are well-equipped, rural India is still developing charging stations.
  • Initial Cost: EVs currently have a higher upfront purchase price compared to petrol variants (though cheaper to own long-term).
  • Charging Time: Refueling takes 5 minutes; charging can take 45 mins (Fast Charge) to 8 hours (Home Charge).
